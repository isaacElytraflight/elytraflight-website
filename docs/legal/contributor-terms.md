# Contributor terms

**The agreement in [CLA.md](CLA.md) is a draft.** It is not in effect. Nothing in this file asks you to sign it. Samantha reviews the draft before Isaac signs off, and an attorney or the Berkeley Law clinic reviews it before anyone is asked to agree.

## What is already in effect

Two outbound licenses are in effect. Isaac chose them on 2026-11-12.

- Code is Apache-2.0. See [LICENSE](../../LICENSE).
- Learning content (questions, explainers, figures, and skill-graph data) is CC BY-SA 4.0. See [LICENSE-CONTENT](../../LICENSE-CONTENT).
- [NOTICE](../../NOTICE) maps paths to those licenses. The CS 180 pages and `public/profile.png` are all rights reserved.
- The name and the logo are not licensed. See [TRADEMARKS.md](../../TRADEMARKS.md).

Copyright rules for what you may submit are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md). They apply now, with or without a signed agreement. Write your own text. Do not copy textbooks, readers, slides, homework, exams, or solution sites. Facts, formulas, and topic lists are fine. Disclose a model if one drafted or edited the text. Disclose pre-existing material and its license in the pull request.

There is no payment for a contribution. The git history is the credit.

## What the draft would add, after review

Once the draft is adopted, a contributor also grants Isaac a standing license, not an assignment, so he can distribute the contribution under the outbound licenses and relicense it to a successor nonprofit. He may edit, reject, or remove it. You must be 18, or a guardian co-signs. You must be able to say that no employer or school claims the work.

Until that adoption, do not treat a pull request as a signed agreement.
